# Voice, music, type, transitions

## Voice

Kokoro 82M, voice `am_michael`, loudnorm target -16 LUFS.

## Music

`warm-acoustic-01` from the OpenReels Pixabay bundle, source id 232912. Loudnorm target -28 LUFS. Playback volume 0.85, with a fade in and a fade out. This replaces the earlier epic-cinematic bed.

## Sound effects

Generated with ffmpeg at 24 kHz. A 260 ms pink-noise whoosh marks a scene change. A 420 ms 587 Hz sine marks the product intro and the close. They are mixed under the voice, not instead of it.

## Type

Inter. Headlines in ink. Kickers in forest. Captions in cream on an ink plate.

## Transitions

Twelve-frame opacity fade on scene content. The paper background stays up.
